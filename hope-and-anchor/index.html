Hope and Anchor

2024
Branding
Identity system, brand guideline

Designed a branding system and a brand guideline for Hope and Anchor, a fictional community-based public library. Public, means that it is open to everyone. Library, means that it is a source of information and literacy.

The slogan of this library is “I love my neighbors, so does my library.” This amplifies the openness of the Hope and Anchor library to the community members.
